# Liddle Puzzles Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 20, 2026

This policy applies to Liddle Puzzles (`fyi.mllab.puzzle`), an Android app developed by Mike Liddle (Liddle Laboratory).

## What the app collects

Liddle Puzzles works offline. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, or internet permission, and it does not collect or send personal information, usage data, device identifiers, or crash reports to the developer or anyone else.

## What stays on your device

The app uses Android's private app storage to save puzzles, progress, notes, move history, solve times, personal bests, and game and appearance settings. Daily Word also saves the local dates you played, your guesses, unfinished draft, and active play time. It uses your device's date and data bundled with the app, not a server.

Saves and settings stay on your device across restarts and compatible updates. To delete them, clear Liddle Puzzles' storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Liddle Puzzles disables Android cloud backup and does not upload or sync saves. Some devices may handle device-to-device transfers separately.

## Sharing a puzzle

When you choose Share, Liddle Puzzles passes a link containing only the puzzle's starting definition to the Android Sharesheet. In app versions that support timed sharing, if you solved the puzzle and the app recorded a solve time, the outgoing message also shows that time as text outside the link. Neither the link nor the message includes your moves, notes, answers, or move history. The messaging app you choose handles the message under its own privacy policy. The recipient's progress stays on their device.

## Website and downloads

Visiting this page or downloading the app is separate from using the app. The [Liddle Laboratory website privacy policy]({{ '/privacy' | relative_url }}) describes this site's data practices. Google Play, other download hosts, Android, and your device manufacturer may process data under their own policies. Liddle Puzzles does not control those services.
