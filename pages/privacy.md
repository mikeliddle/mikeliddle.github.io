**Effective date:** September 8, 2026

Liddle Laboratory respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website and related applications or services (collectively, the "Services"), why we collect it, and how it is handled.

## Information We Collect

We collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the Services. This may include:

- Information you provide when using a feature of the Services, such as account, contact, or content information required for that feature.
- Technical information needed to operate the Services, such as device or browser type, operating system, approximate location derived from an IP address, request timestamps, and error or diagnostic data.
- Basic aggregate telemetry, such as feature usage, page views, performance measurements, and general usage trends. We use this information to understand how the Services are used and to improve reliability and functionality.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not collect personal information for targeted advertising.

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Provide and support the Services and their requested features.
- Maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot errors.
- Measure aggregate usage and improve performance, reliability, and usability.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

## Sharing and Service Providers

We may share information with service providers that host, operate, analyze, or secure the Services, but only as needed for them to perform those services on our behalf. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of users, the public, or the Services.

## Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Your Choices

You may choose not to provide optional information. However, information required for a feature may be necessary to use that feature. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

## Third-Party Links

The Services may link to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the Services change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised. Material changes will be communicated through the Services when appropriate.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Liddle Laboratory through the repository linked on this site.
